## 3 · Typography

**Momo Trust Sans** (variable, `wght` 200–800) and **Luckiest Guy** (static
400), self-hosted. Converted TTF → WOFF2: **236 KB → 95 KB**.

Every text button is `.btn-type` — Momo Trust Sans at rest, snapping to
Luckiest Guy for as long as the press is held, with the ledge going plum. One
class rather than two utilities so the pair cannot drift, and hung off
`:active` so keyboard and touch come free.

## 4 · Defects found and fixed

The ones worth remembering, because each has a general lesson.

### Build and tooling

**`sharp` runs `trim` before `extract`.** A trim first moves the very
coordinates a crop is expressed in. The `promise-owl` crop failed outright —
trimming took the ribbon from 224px tall to 183 before a 224px extract was
applied. Every entry with an `extract` box now also sets `trim: false`.

**Tailwind v4 tree-shakes `@keyframes` declared inside `@theme`** unless a
matching `animate-*` utility is used. The feather-fall animation needs a
per-feather duration and delay, so it is driven from inline `animation`
shorthand — and had to move out of `@theme` or it never shipped.

**Vite's watcher glob was eating new art.** `server.watch.ignored` was a bare
`**/assets/**`, which also matched `public/assets/**`. Vite caches the
public-directory listing at boot and only refreshes it from watcher events, so
every file `npm run assets` wrote while the server ran fell through to the SPA
fallback and rendered as a broken image until restart. Now anchored to the
repo-root `assets/` only.

### Layering and transforms

**Tailwind's `scale` property is applied before Motion's `transform`.** With
`-scale-x-100` on the class list, Motion's leftward `x` ran in already-mirrored
coordinates and the jet flew *right*. Moving the flip into Motion's transform
(`scaleX: -1`, which lands after `translateX` in its ordering) fixed the
direction.

**`getBoundingClientRect()` returns the *scaled* box.** The card carousel
measured its step off it, so a shrunken neighbour's width fed back into the
centring maths and parked the active card 75px off. `offsetWidth`/`offsetLeft`
ignore transforms.

**A percentage `bottom` inside a flex column resolves against a height that
only exists after layout runs** — it was landing the wizard at the *top* of his
column. Flex placement needs no such resolution.

### Assets

**`logo.png` carries an opaque white plate**, cropped from the white navbar
export. Invisible in the nav; a white rectangle everywhere else, and the
footer's `brightness-0 invert` turned it into a solid white block. Fixed with a
border flood-fill (`scripts/make-logo-alpha.mjs`) that feathers the rim and
keeps white *enclosed* by the mark.

**`cloud-wisp.webp` runs off its right edge** — max alpha 255 there against
13–16 on the other three sides, so every copy ends on a ruled vertical cut. It
went unnoticed under the hand-off's white wash; over open sky it read as a pale
rectangle. Faded on the *image*, not the layer — a layer mask only reaches its
own boundary and misses copies sitting mid-layer.

**`cloud-puff` was cropped into the export's dirty zone**, so the crop's own
bottom edge drew a line across the sky.

**Two "different" careers photos are the same frame** — `Group 2087325405.png`
and `...406.png` differ by 5 bytes across 2 MB, all under a delta of 16.

**The card carousel was cropping 8% off one photo.** `flock-wide` was 1174×684
(1.716), `flock-candid` 822×573 (1.435), and one shared `aspect-*` with
`object-cover` took the difference off the candid's top and bottom. Both are
now cropped to their card region at ~1.986 and the markup crops nothing.

**`flock-wide` has its own caption bar painted in** (the teal pill runs y
690–765), so a crop that keeps it renders the role and button twice — once in
pixels, once in markup.

### Layout and a11y

**`Reveal`'s default viewport insets its trigger 12% up from the bottom**, and
nothing in the page's last 12% can satisfy that. Scrolled fully down, the
footer's link row sat at 858 of a 900 viewport — past the 792 line — and stayed
at `opacity: 0` permanently.

**~890px of layout shift** from a lazy-loaded pilot with no intrinsic size. It
was the largest shift on the page and it landed on the last thing anyone
scrolls to. Declaring its dimensions cut total page shift from ~1000px to 360.

**The hero composition outgrew its own section.** Measured in `vw` against a
flat `200svh`, on a wide-but-short window the broom owl finished 315px past the
section's end and `overflow-hidden` sliced it mid-bird. Height is now
`max(200svh, 122vw)`.
